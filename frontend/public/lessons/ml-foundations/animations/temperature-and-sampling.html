Temperature & Sampling

An LLM doesn’t “know” the next word — it bets on it. It scores every possible next token, then rolls a weighted dice to pick one. Temperature controls how wild that dice roll gets.

Temperature = how much the model spreads its bets before picking the next token. Low temperature concentrates everything on the favourite; high temperature spreads the bets out, so long shots start winning.

Cold T ≈ 0.2

Plays it safe. Nearly every pick is the top answer — great for facts and code, dull for stories.

Original T = 1.0

The model’s probabilities, untouched. The favourite usually wins, but surprises happen.

Hot T ≈ 2.0

Bets spread almost evenly. Creative and unpredictable — and sometimes just wrong (Berlin is not in France!).
