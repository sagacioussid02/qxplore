Words that mean similar things live close together

An embedding turns every word into a list of numbers — a vector. A model learns those numbers from how words are actually used, so words that show up in similar contexts end up with similar vectors.

Flattened onto a 2D map, that means neighbors = similar meaning. The words below fly into their neighborhoods — tap any word to meet its closest neighbors.

Tap a word — its three nearest neighbors light up, with a similarity score for each. On this map the score comes from distance (closer = higher); real models compare the angle between vectors instead.

Word arithmetic: directions carry meaning

Because relationships become directions in the vector, you can do sums with words. The classic example:

king − man + woman ≈ queen

A toy sketch: this map gives every word just 2 numbers so you can see it. Real embeddings (Word2Vec, and the ones inside today's language models) use hundreds or thousands of numbers per word — same idea, far richer neighborhoods.
